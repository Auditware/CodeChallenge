# The Enchanted Compound Fork - Audit Wizard Find the Bug Challenge #4

Welcome, noble auditors! Today, you face a trial of arcane knowledge as you delve into the depths of a mysterious Compound Fork contract. Hidden within this seemingly benign contract is a vulnerability that allows a devious blackhat to siphon funds from unsuspecting depositors. Your mission is to to uncover and exploit this flaw.

Correctly finding the bug will unlock a commemorative badge in Audit Wizard. The badge will be added to your auditor profile. This is just the start—participate in each challenge to build your collection!

---

This challenge by [Tomer](https://twitter.com/forefy), the creator of [eburger](https://github.com/forefy/eburger), includes a Compound Fork. A Compound Fork is a decentralized finance (DeFi) platform derived from the original Compound protocol, enabling users to lend and borrow cryptocurrencies.

Happy hunting!
